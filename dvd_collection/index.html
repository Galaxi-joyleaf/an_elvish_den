DVD collection

I've been collecting DVDs for about 2 years now. I grew up watching them so my first step was to regroup all our old ones in one spot. Although half of them were children shows and movies, the other half were classics : a couple Marvel movies, the first Matrix, the Lord of the Rings, etc.

My next step was to try and complete my Marvel collection. I was able to find quite a few in thrift stores and flea markets and bought a couple that were on sale in local stores. I haven't completed it yet, there are so many !

Now, I'm just trying to collect all my favourite movies (especially the rarer ones) so I can rewatch them whenever I want !

It's a little harder with TV shows though because of their price. It isn't easy to find the ones I want while thrifting too especially since the thrift stores around me are very small.


I do own a couple blu-rays but I don't have a portable player for them (apart from our PS5) and I'm satisfied with the quality dvds offer.
